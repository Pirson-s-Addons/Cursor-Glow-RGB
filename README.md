## What it does

**Cursor Glow RGB** (in game: **RGBCursor**) draws a glowing aura on top of your mouse cursor so you never lose it in a busy fight. You pick its shape, color, size and position, and it follows the cursor everywhere on screen.

## Features

- **8 cursor styles**: Glow, Circle, Ring, Dot, Crosshair, Reticle, Corners and Diamond.
- **13 color styles**: Blue, Cyan, Green, Lime, Yellow, Orange, Red, Pink, Purple, Magenta, White, an animated **RGB** cycle, and **Custom** (any color and opacity from the color picker).
- **Size** from 1 to 100 and **X / Y offset** from -100 to 100 relative to the cursor.
- Optionally **hide the aura while the left or right mouse button is held** (right click is on by default).
- **Default Values** button to reset everything.
- Translated into 20 languages. Settings are saved per account (`RGBCursorDB`).

## Installation

1. Download the zip from the [latest release](https://github.com/Pirson-s-Addons/Cursor-Glow-RGB/releases/latest) or from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/cursor-glow-rgb).
2. Extract the `RGBCursor` folder into the AddOns folder of your game version:
   - Retail: `World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns/`
   - Classic (Mists): `World of Warcraft/_classic_/Interface/AddOns/`
   - Classic Era: `World of Warcraft/_classic_era_/Interface/AddOns/`
   - TBC Anniversary: `World of Warcraft/_anniversary_/Interface/AddOns/`
   - WoW Forever: `World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/`
3. Restart WoW and enable the addon.

## Usage

- `/rgbc` or `/rgbcursor` opens the settings.
- The settings live in the game's **Options → AddOns → RGB Cursor → General**. The **RGB Cursor** page itself shows the version, links and commands.

## Notes

The folder must be named `RGBCursor` (its TOC files are `RGBCursor*.toc`). It also ships TOCs for older clients (2.4.3, 3.3.5, 4.3.4, 5.4.8, WoD, Legion, BfA and Shadowlands).
